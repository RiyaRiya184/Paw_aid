Frequently asked questions

How do I report a dog?

Go to the Report a Dog page, describe what you saw, mark the severity, and share the location. You'll get a tracking ID once it's submitted.

How do I check the status of my report?

Use the Track a Report page with your tracking ID — no account needed. If you're logged in, you can also see all your reports under "My Reports".

How long does a rescue usually take?

It depends on severity and NGO availability, but most cases are picked up within 24–48 hours of being reported.

I run an NGO. How do I join Paw Aid?

Register using the NGO option on the Register page. Our team verifies each organization before activating an account.

Still need help?

Reach out on the Contact Us page and we'll get back to you.
