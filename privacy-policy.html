Privacy policy

Your privacy matters to us

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you file a report, register, or contact us, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, phone number, email, and the information in your report — so we can connect you with the right NGO.

How we use it

Your contact details are shared only with the NGO assigned to a case, so their team can follow up if needed. We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Report tracking IDs

Tracking IDs let anyone check a report's status without logging in, but they don't expose your name, phone number, or exact address.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by reaching out through our Contact Us page.
